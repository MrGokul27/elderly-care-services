OUR ORIGINS

A personal pledge to raise home care standards

"Stackly Care Services was born from my own struggle to find reliable, high-quality care for my aged parents. I wanted more than just basic nursing; I wanted genuine companionship, respect, and cognitive stimulation for them. That search defined our agency's standard: we only recruit caregivers we would trust with our own family."

Evelyn Vance
| Founder & CEO

OUR ROADMAP

Our journey through the years

How we grew from a small community service to a nationwide registered care provider.

2016
Founding of Stackly Care

Began local support activities with only three registered nurse operators in upstate New York.

2019
Registered NDIS Provider

Achieved formal healthcare compliance registration, enabling government-subsidized care packages.

2022
Specialized Dementia Launch

Partnered with memory retention institutions to roll out certified Alzheimer's care training modules.

2026
10,000+ Families Served

Proudly expanded clinical operations nationwide, supported by over 500 credentialed caregivers.
