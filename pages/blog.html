Q&A CORNER

Senior Care Q&A with Head Nurse Sarah

Replies to complex client questions submitted by families regarding senior care methods.

Q: "How can we encourage a senior who refuses home nursing?"

A: Start slowly by introducing a "companion" rather than a clinical nurse. Match the caregiver based on common hobbies, like gardening or books, to build friendship first before adding clinical checks.

Q: "What are early warning signs of cognitive decline?"

A: Sudden changes in memory, repeating questions, confusion with dates or times, struggling with familiar kitchen tasks, and mood variations. Early consultation with a physician is highly advised.
